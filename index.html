Why snag before handover

The best time to get defects fixed is before you've signed.

Once you accept handover, every defect becomes a negotiation. A documented snag list, submitted before or right at handover, puts the burden back on the developer, at their cost.

New doesn't mean finished

Rushed handovers are common. A typical new apartment has 40–80 defects, most invisible to a homeowner on a 20-minute walk-through.

The fixes are free. Finding them isn't.

Developers fix reported defects during the defects liability period. If they aren't reported, you fix them yourself, after the warranty runs out.

Protect what you paid for

A hidden leak or bad waterproofing can cost far more than an inspection. Tenants and buyers will find these issues eventually. Better that you find them first.

Questions

Good to know.

When should I book the inspection?

Ideally after the developer issues your handover notice and before you sign the handover documents. That's when you have the most leverage. If you've already moved in, an inspection is still worth doing while you're inside the defects liability period.

Do I need to be there?

No. Many of our clients are overseas investors. We can coordinate access with the developer or your agent and walk you through the findings on a video call.

What do I do with the report?

Forward it to your developer's handover or customer care team. It's written so their contractors can act on it directly. When they say the work is done, book a re-inspection to check every item has actually been fixed.

Do you inspect ready (secondary) properties?

Yes. We inspect properties before purchase and before tenancy too. The checklist is the same, and the report helps you negotiate on price or set the condition at move-in.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer or card payment link once your booking is confirmed. Nothing is payable upfront to make an enquiry.

Will you fix the defects for us?

No, and that's deliberate. To stay independent we never quote for repairs on a property we've inspected. Under your warranty, the developer is responsible for fixing them.
