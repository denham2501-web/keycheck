FAQ

Common questions.

When should I book the inspection?

Ideally after your developer issues the handover notice and before you sign the handover documents. That's when you have the most leverage. If you've already moved in, an inspection is still worth doing within your defects liability period.

Do I need to be there?

No. Many of our clients are overseas investors. We can coordinate access with the developer or your agent and go through the findings with you on a video call.

What do I do with the report?

Send it to your developer's handover or customer care team. It's written so their contractors can work from it directly. When they say the work is complete, book a re-inspection to confirm.

Do you inspect resale properties?

Yes. The checklist is the same, and the report helps you negotiate on price or record the condition at move-in.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer or card payment link once your booking is confirmed. There's nothing to pay to make an enquiry.

Will you fix the defects?

No, and that's deliberate. To stay independent we never quote for repairs on a property we've inspected. Under your warranty, the developer is responsible for the fixes.
