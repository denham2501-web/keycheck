Inspector's summary

Finishes are generally good, but three items should be fixed before you accept handover: the master shower tray falls away from the drain, the kitchen sink waste is leaking into the cabinet base, and one bedroom socket has reversed polarity. The rest are typical finishing defects that the developer's contractor can close out in a single visit.

Defect register (extract)

#014 · Shower tray falls away from drainCritical
Master bathroom · Plumbing & tiling

Water pools about 300 mm from the drain after a 60-second run, with a measured back-fall of about 4 mm. Ongoing ponding risks breaking down the grout and getting past the waterproofing.

Recommended: re-lay the tray tiles to a 1:80 minimum fall to the drain. Flood test after.

#022 · Sink waste leaking into cabinet baseCritical
Kitchen · Plumbing

The waste coupling under the sink is loose. Water is pooling on the cabinet base and the moisture meter reads 38% at the board edge, so the MDF is already swelling.

Recommended: re-seal the coupling and replace the cabinet base panel.

#031 · Hollow-sounding floor tiles (6 no.)Major
Living room, near balcony slider · Tiling

Six tiles sound hollow when tapped, which means poor adhesive coverage. One already has a hairline crack. Hollow tiles often crack under load or heat.

Recommended: lift and re-bed the affected tiles with full adhesive coverage.

#047 · Door frame paint chipped at headMinor
Bedroom 2 · Painting & finishes

Paint chipped on the frame head (about 40 mm) with bare timber showing. Probably impact damage during construction.

Recommended: fill, sand and repaint to match.
